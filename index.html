The throughline

I studied History and International Studies, then took a Master's in Peace and Conflict Resolution, and spent three years at the Lagos State Citizens Mediation Bureau sitting across from people in genuine disagreement, trying to get them to a workable outcome. It's a job made entirely of listening carefully and building trust before you build anything else.

I taught myself software development the same way — by building things people actually needed and fixing what broke. I now work across the full stack: React, Next.js, Node.js, Supabase, and enough time spent wiring up Nigerian payment processors to know Paystack's and Flutterwave's edge cases by heart.

Longer term, I'm working toward a PhD that ties the two together — building better digital tools for mediators and peacebuilders.
